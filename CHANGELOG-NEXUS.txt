Zone Companions - Changelog
Short version. The full, technical changelog is CHANGELOG.md.


VERSION 1.1.0

New: Hire companions from an NPC for money
- A hireable-labour contact now stands in the hub bar.
- Walk up to him and press F (the normal Interact key) to open the hire menu.
- Three tiers: Rookie 2,500 - Veteran 6,000 - Elite 12,000 roubles.
- Higher tiers really are tougher, not just more expensive.
- W/S to choose, Enter to buy, Backspace to close.

New: Your companions' eyes now actually work
- Enemies, loot containers and the companions themselves are visible when a
  companion can see them, even if you cannot.
- Each companion's view cone clears the fog of war, exactly like your own.
- Companion flashlights are now visible outside your own view cone.
- No coloured overlays or boxes - the map simply opens up where your squad is
  looking.
- Optional map pins for companions who are off-screen or behind fog (off by
  default).

New: The config finally updates itself
- There are now two files. csq_config.ini is a generated reference listing every
  setting with its default and a description - it is rewritten every launch, so
  a mod update's new settings always show up in it.
- csq_config_user.ini is yours. Put only the lines you want to change in it. The
  mod never overwrites it.
- Nothing to do when you update: your old settings are moved into the new user
  file for you on the first launch.
- Edit the wrong file by mistake and the mod moves your changes across for you
  and tells you in the log. Nothing is lost.

Changed
- F now opens the recruiter menu. Free debug spawning moved to F6 and only
  works with debug mode switched on.
- 97 settings across 11 sections, up from 61 across 9. Nothing was removed.

Fixed
- Companions were invisible whenever they left your own view cone.
- Companion flashlights produced no light out of your sight.
- Loot containers next to a companion stayed hidden.

Known issue
- Wall shadows inside ground a companion revealed are still drawn relative to
  your position, not theirs. The ground is revealed correctly; only the
  direction the shadows fall is wrong.

Upgrading from 1.0.0
- Nothing to do. Your existing settings are carried over automatically and your
  saves are unaffected.


VERSION 1.0.0

First release.
- Up to 3 persistent companions, saved with your character.
- Real vanilla combat AI and real pathfinding - they take cover, flank, shoot
  and reload, and route around buildings.
- Formation following that keeps up with your actual speed, and idle roaming.
- Field medic: a companion breaks off to patch you up, and drops everything to
  reach you when you are badly hurt.
- Two-way friendly fire protection.
- Squad panel with names, HP, field dressings and current mode.
- Injuries carry from one raid to the next.
- 61 settings in a fully commented config file, plus logging for bug reports.
